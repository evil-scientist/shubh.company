Our Vision

The society we are working toward.

A society where every child can learn and grow up healthy, where no girl is married as a child, where families are free of addiction, and where elderly people and children with special needs live with dignity and care.

What we are working toward

  • Every child in school and learning, including children in slums and villages.
  • No girl married as a child.
  • Families free from alcohol, tobacco and drug addiction.
  • Adolescents who understand their health and can ask for help.
  • Healthy food on every plate, and no child left malnourished.
  • Elderly people who live with dignity and company.
  • Children with special needs included and valued in every community.
